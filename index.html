Principles

Seven rules Septakis works by

  1. Offline friendly

    A tool you depend on shouldn’t depend on a connection. Ours run on your own machines, rely on no service of ours, and treat the network as optional.

  2. Wide compatibility

    The best tool is the one that fits what’s already there. We build on open standards and common ground, so nothing has to be torn down to make room for us.

  3. Reduce toil

    Repetitive work is a job for machines; judgment is a job for people. Our tools take the first off your hands, so you have more time for the second.

  4. Test everything

    Dependable software comes from testing. Every release we make is tested against real-world projects before it ships, so it does what you expect when you rely on it.

  5. Built to last

    Tools become part of how you work. We change ours carefully, keep existing setups working, and never break what you rely on without warning.

  6. Listen and respond

    The people who use our tools shape what they become. We read every report, answer quickly, and show what changed because of what you told us.

  7. Make the world a safer place

    Safer software is good for everyone. We keep the foundations open, make the rest free for those who build in the open, and share what we learn.

vexproof is built to all seven. Anything we make after it will be too.
